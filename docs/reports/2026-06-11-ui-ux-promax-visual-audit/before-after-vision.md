# Before and After Vision

## Current Experience

The local product looks ambitious and feature-rich, but its visual language competes with itself. Glass, gradients, orbs, marquees, 3D, sticky scrolling, typing, radar, pulses, and emoji all ask for attention. Portal value is present, yet navigation and duplicated landing showcases make discovery feel like browsing inventory. The live site further weakens trust by presenting an older brand state.

## Target Experience

NexaLearn should feel like a focused smart learning operating system:

- One unmistakable hero message.
- One main next action.
- Portal discovery organized by learner goal.
- A consistent platform shell with distinct portal accents.
- Calm motion that explains state and progression.
- Arabic that feels native and English that is genuinely LTR.
- Trust surfaces that use one issuer and one brand identity.

## User Feeling

**Before:** “There is a lot here; where should I start, and is this the current product?”

**After:** “This platform understands my goal, shows the right starting point, and looks trustworthy enough to register.”

## Final Visual Identity

- Deep neutral surfaces with controlled glass, not glass on every element.
- NexaLearn as the product name; Darhous as endorsed creator/company according to one documented lockup.
- Cairo/IBM Plex Arabic and Geist/IBM Plex English with a documented scale.
- SVG portal marks with fixed geometry.
- One accent and one motif per portal.
- Green reserved for success/progress, not generic decoration.
- Motion used for entrance, progress, and feedback only.

## Publish-Ready Definition

The platform becomes publish-ready when:

1. The live URL shows the approved NexaLearn build.
2. Arabic and English routes are complete and directionally correct.
3. Every key task is reachable through a simplified navigation model.
4. The homepage has one clear conversion flow.
5. Mobile touch, overflow, and fixed controls pass real-device QA.
6. Auth, certificate, error, loading, empty, OG, and footer surfaces share one brand system.
