# Concept Comparison

**Date:** 2026-06-11

This document compares the five cinematic preview concepts created for the NexaLearn homepage redesign.

| Metric | Concept 1 (AI Command Center) | Concept 2 (The Learning Journey) | Concept 3 (Silent Authority) | Concept 4 (Portal Constellation) | Concept 5 (Goal Gateway) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Design Quality** | High (Dashboard UI, immersive) | High (Narrative flow) | Very High (Clean SaaS, premium) | Very High (Cinematic, glowing) | High (Interactive flow) |
| **Motion Level** | High (Status bars, widgets) | Medium (Scroll reveals) | Low (Subtle hovers) | Very High (Orbital 3D-like) | Medium (Slide transitions) |
| **Conversion Strength**| Medium (Oriented on info) | High (Outcomes focused) | High (Trust-first) | Low (Exploration focused) | Very High (Personalized flow) |
| **Implementation Risk**| High (Complex dashboard layout) | Medium (Content variations) | Low (Simple standard layout) | Very High (GPU intensive) | High (Viewport locking state) |
| **Mobile Risk** | High (Complex stacking) | Medium | Low | Very High (Touch interaction on orbs) | Low (Mobile-friendly selector) |
| **Best Use Case** | Showing platform capability | Guiding beginners | Building corporate trust | High-impact visual differentiation| Optimizing user onboarding |
| **Recommended Review**| 3rd | 4th | 1st | 5th | 2nd |

## Recommended Review Order
We recommend the owner review the concepts in this order:
1. **Silent Authority**: Establishes the baseline of trust and premium typography.
2. **Goal Gateway**: Showcases the power of personalization immediately.
3. **AI Command Center**: Demonstrates the depth and ecosystem mapping.
4. **The Learning Journey**: Shows how narrative can guide the user.
5. **Portal Constellation**: Ends with the most visually ambitious, cinematic exploration.
